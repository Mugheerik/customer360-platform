# Customer360 Platform Roadmap

## Vision

Customer360 is being developed as an enterprise platform engineering laboratory that explores how scalable, customer-centric software systems are designed, built, and evolved.

Rather than focusing on a single business application, the platform is intended to grow into a collection of reusable capabilities that support identity, customer management, analytics, automation, and intelligent business services.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every milestone should:

* Deliver a meaningful business capability.
* Strengthen the platform architecture.
* Follow production engineering practices.
* Improve maintainability, testability, observability, or extensibility.

# Milestone Summary

## v0.1.0 — Platform Foundation

Established the technical foundation of the platform.

Key outcomes:

* FastAPI application
* PostgreSQL integration
* SQLAlchemy ORM
* Alembic migrations
* Customer CRUD
* Automated testing
* Continuous Integration

---

## v0.2.0 — Identity & Security

Introduced authentication and authorization capabilities.

Key outcomes:

* JWT authentication
* User management
* Role-based authorization
* Standardized exception handling
* Improved test coverage
* Semantic versioning
* Verified commit signing

---

## v0.3.0 — Customer Domain

The next milestone focuses on strengthening the core customer domain.

Planned objectives:

* Rich customer services
* Advanced validation
* Search and filtering
* Pagination
* Domain-focused business rules
* Improved repository capabilities

---

## Future Milestones

As the platform matures, additional capabilities will be introduced, including:

* Shared platform services
* Workflow automation
* Event-driven communication
* Analytics and reporting
* Cloud-native deployment
* AI-assisted business capabilities

Each milestone will build upon the existing architecture while maintaining clear module boundaries and engineering discipline.

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

Customer360 evolves by building **platform capabilities**, not isolated features.

Every release should leave the platform:

* More reusable
* More maintainable
* More testable
* More extensible

This philosophy guides the long-term evolution of the platform and ensures that each milestone contributes to a stronger engineering foundation.
